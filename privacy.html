Rats — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026 Status: Public beta

Rats ("Rats," "we," "us") is a skatepark stampbook and social app for skateboarders. This policy describes exactly what the app collects, why, where it goes, and how you control it. It is written to match what the code actually does. Where a practice needs a lawyer's review before public launch it is marked [ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED].

Rats is a free beta. It has no ads, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell your data.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The Rats app team is the data controller. Our infrastructure provider (Supabase) is a data processor acting on our instructions. [ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED] — confirm the controlling legal entity, business address, and a monitored contact address before launch, and whether an EU/UK representative or DPA is required for your user base.

2. What we collect and why

Account information — email address and username. Your email is used only for authentication (sign-in, email confirmation, password reset). It is stored by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) and is never shown to other users. Your username and (optional) display name, bio, and profile photo are shown to other signed-in users. Stored in: auth.users (email), public.profiles (username, display name, bio, avatar URL).

Location — precise device GPS, used at the moment of an action. Rats uses your device's location to: - verify you are physically at a skatepark before you can "stamp" it or post a clip there (a ~150–200 meter geofence check); - count a park visit toward "Local" status (you become a Local after 15 separate visits, each at least 18 hours apart) — checked only while the app is open in the foreground; - detect the park you are at so you can join its battle queue; - show nearby parks on the map and, with your permission, center the map on you.

We store the coordinate captured at the instant you stamp, post, or log a visit, as anti-cheat evidence that the action really happened at the park. These precise coordinates are never exposed through the app's API to other users — a public check-in reveals the skatepark, not your exact position. The raw visit-location log is readable only by you. We do not run any continuous or background location tracking. Stored in: stamps.checkin_location, posts.author_location, park_visits.checkin_location. Coordinates are also sent to Google (see §4) to render maps and, for a new park submission, to look up an address.

Photos and videos you choose to upload. Skate clips (video), profile pictures, park-submission photos, and optional bug-report screenshots. The app only ever accesses the single file you actively pick; it does not scan or upload your library in the background. Before upload, we re-encode images to strip embedded metadata, including any GPS coordinates in the photo's EXIF. Stored in: Supabase Storage buckets (posts, avatars, parks, bug-screenshots). Uploaded media is served from public, unguessable URLs so it can appear in the feed. [ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED] — see note on video metadata in §8.

Content you create. Captions, comments, your bio, stamps, follows, blocks, mutes, battle records, and crowns. These power the social features and are shown to other signed-in users according to your settings.

Push notification token. If you enable notifications, we store a device push token so we can send banners for likes, comments, follows, mentions, and battle/park events. Turning any notification type off in Edit Profile stops the underlying notification from being generated at all — not just hidden. Stored in: push_tokens (readable only by you).

Bug reports. If you submit one, we store your description, your device OS and version, and any screenshot you attach.

Technical/server logs. Like any hosted service, our provider (Supabase) and Google process your IP address and basic request metadata to deliver the service and for security. We do not use this for advertising or profiling.

We deliberately do not collect: date of birth, phone number, contacts, precise background location, advertising identifiers, or behavioral analytics.

3. How we use your information

Only to operate the app's features: verifying park visits, showing your content to the people you have chosen to share it with, sending notifications you opted into, ranking Locals/Rat Kings, and reviewing reported content for safety. We do not use your data for advertising and do not sell it.

4. Third parties who receive data

  • Supabase — our database, file storage, and authentication provider (data processor). Receives everything above as our backend.
  • Google Maps / Places — receives location coordinates and address text to render maps and resolve park addresses. Governed by Google's own privacy policy. The address-lookup call is proxied through our server so our Google API key is not exposed.
  • Expo / Apple Push (APNs) / Google (FCM) — deliver push notifications when you enable them; they receive your push token and the notification content.

We do not share your data with any other third party, and none of these are permitted to use it for their own purposes beyond providing their service. [ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED] — confirm each processor's terms/DPA and disclose any additional sub-processors before launch.

5. Your controls and rights

In the app you can: edit or delete your bio, avatar, posts, and comments; choose who can comment on your posts; make your battle log private; block or mute other users; and turn off any notification type.

Account and data deletion. Edit Profile → Delete my account (confirmed with your password) immediately: deletes your posts, comments, likes, follows, blocks, mutes, stamps, visit history, push tokens, and uploaded files; anonymizes your profile to a non-identifying placeholder so other players' battle history stays intact; and disables your login so it can never be used again. This cannot be undone.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data, or to object to processing. You can exercise most of these in-app; for anything else, contact us (§9). [ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED] — GDPR/UK GDPR/CCPA-CPRA specifics, verification process, and response timelines.

6. Data retention

Content is retained until you delete it or delete your account. On account deletion, personal data is removed or anonymized as described in §5. Server security logs are retained by our provider for a limited period per their policy. [ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED] — set and document specific retention periods for backups and logs.

7. Children

Rats is intended for users aged 13 and older, and skateboarding content can appeal to younger users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. We do not currently ask for date of birth or run an age gate. If we learn we have collected data from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it. See the Terms of Service for the eligibility requirement. [ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED] — COPPA (US), UK Age-Appropriate Design Code, GDPR age-of-consent, and app-store age ratings almost certainly require an age gate and/or parental-consent flow before public launch; this is a launch blocker to resolve with counsel.

8. Security

Access to your data is enforced at the database with row-level security and column-level permissions: precise location is never returned to other users, only you and admins can see privileged data, and administrative actions are authorized on the server and logged. Media buckets restrict which file types can be uploaded. Passwords are handled entirely by our authentication provider; we never see them.

Known limitation (disclosed honestly): image uploads have their metadata stripped, but video files are not yet metadata-stripped — a clip could still carry a camera-embedded location tag in its container. We mitigate this by never exposing per-post precise coordinates through the API and by the park geofence, and metadata stripping for video is on the pre-launch list. [ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED] / engineering follow-up.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.
